Carillon is a Crillion-like game, inspired by 2001 hit Frozen Fruits, made in 13 days for the Revival Jam 2024 organized by the Society of Play.

It features dozens of unique puzzles.

Carillion is published with its puzzle editor, and the player can create and share his own creations.

Carillion is available for free on WebGamer.io, Viverse and Itch.io
